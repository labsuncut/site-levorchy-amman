Bleu Souveraine

$1,3961,396 دولار

A study in precision and presence.

Bleu Souveraine is a three-piece composition defined by structure and fluidity. The strap bodice is sculpted with geometric gold embroidery, tracing the silhouette with deliberate refinement. It reveals strength through symmetry, restraint through detail.

The wide-leg trousers fall with effortless movement, elongating the frame in pure, uninterrupted lines. Layered above, the abaya introduces softness its fluid drape contrasted by fine gold accents that echo the architectural motif beneath.

Each element stands independently. Together, they form a silhouette of quiet command.

Imperial blue, reimagined with modern discipline.
