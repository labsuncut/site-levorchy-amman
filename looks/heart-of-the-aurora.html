Aurora Bordeauxأورورا بوردو

Heart of the Aurora

4,560 JOD4,560 د.أ

Commanding the runway as the centerpiece of the Aurora Bordeaux collection, “The Heart of the Aurora” captures the essence of Levorchy’s universe bold, regal, and luminous. The gown unfolds in deep maroon, scarlet, and crystal gradients, echoing the sky’s transformation from dusk to dawn.

Its sculpted bodice, crafted in maroon and tied into a structured bow, radiates sensual strength, while the voluminous skirt cascades into layers of hand-embroidered sequins and crystal beading that shimmer like celestial waves. Representing blooming energy at the core of the aurora’s light.
