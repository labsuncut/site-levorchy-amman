La reina

2,700 JOD2,700 د.أ

A celebration of contrast and couture detail, this gown pairs luminous fuchsia draping with a delicately patterned blush panel, creating a composition that feels both regal and contemporary.

The central panel is enriched with ornamental embroidery, intricate beadwork, and crystal-like accents, forming a continuous decorative motif inspired by classical textile artistry. The detailing extends through the upper section, allowing the pattern to become part of the structure of the gown rather than simply an embellishment.

Around it, fluid fuchsia satin wraps and falls in generous folds, creating movement and a sense of effortless drama. The contrast between the richly detailed center and the smooth surrounding fabric allows each element to stand out, while the sheen of the satin responds beautifully to changing light.
