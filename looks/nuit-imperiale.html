Bleu Imperialبلو إمبريال

Nuit Impériale

1,375 JOD1,375 د.أ

An evening defined by depth.

Nuit Impériale unfolds in layers of midnight blue. The strapless bodice is entirely hand-embellished with fine stones, creating a surface that shimmers like a constellation against the night. Sculpted and precise, it frames the silhouette with quiet intensity.

Wide-leg trousers extend the line with fluid control, balancing structure with softness.

Draped above, the cover introduces contrast through velvet patchwork a rich oval panel bordered with delicate beading, falling into vertical lines that elongate and refine the movement. The interplay of matte and light, texture and glow, creates dimension without excess.

It is eveningwear reduced to essence.
