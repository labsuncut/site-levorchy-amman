Rosé d' amour

$2,6792,679 دولار

A study in contrast, movement, and refined couture craftsmanship, this creation pairs a muted sage base with a vivid rose accent for a look that feels both elegant and unexpected.

The embellishment is intentionally concentrated, with hand applied crystals, beads, and jewel like motifs adding points of light across the piece. Delicate suspended details create subtle movement along the upper section, while scattered crystal accents continue through the rose draping, giving the design a sense of continuity from top to hem.

Soft folds and asymmetric draping shape the composition with effortless fluidity, while the long contrasting panels introduce a dramatic sense of movement as they trail behind the wearer. As the fabric shifts, the crystals catch the light in small flashes, revealing different textures and reflections from every angle.

قطعة بالأخضر المريمي والوردي الزاهي، بتفاصيل كريستالية مطرّزة يدوياً وزينة معلّقة تتحرّك بخفّة مع كل ضوء.
