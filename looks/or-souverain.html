Bleu Imperialبلو إمبريال

Or Souverain

1,200 JOD1,200 د.أ

An ode to light

Or Souverain is a three-piece ensemble built around brilliance and restraint. The strapless bodice is entirely hand-embellished with gold stones, forming a luminous surface that captures and reflects light with quiet intensity. Sculpted cleanly across the neckline, it feels both modern and timeless.

The tailored trousers introduce depth in contrast, grounding the radiance with fluid precision.

Layered above, the abaya falls in soft champagne tones, its sleeves finished with refined detailing subtle accents that echo the gold beneath without overpowering it.

طقم من ثلاث قطع: صدّ ذهبي مطرّز يدوياً بالكامل، بنطال مفصّل، وعباءة بلون الشمباني بتفاصيل راقية على الأكمام.
