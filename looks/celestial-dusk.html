Celestial Dusk

3,500 JOD3,500 د.أ

The Hero Gown of Aurora Bordeaux

The Celestial Dusk gown stands as the radiant centerpiece of the Aurora Bordeaux collection a masterful expression of sensuality and sophistication. Designed by Norjan Al Kharouf, the piece embodies the collection’s core philosophy: the meeting of light and shadow, grace and strength.

Draped in luminous champagne silk with rich Bordeaux undertones, the gown’s fluid ruching delicately contours the body, evoking the movement of twilight waves against the horizon. A deep halter neckline and a daring front slit reveal both confidence and allure, while the cascading train captures the drama of the setting sun fading into night.

Celestial Dusk is not merely a dress it is a statement of power, poise, and timeless femininity
