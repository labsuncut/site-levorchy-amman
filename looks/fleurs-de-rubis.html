Fleurs de rubis

2,900 JOD2,900 د.أ

A celebration of color and contemporary floral craftsmanship, this fuchsia couture creation transforms delicate blossoms into sculptural elements that seem to bloom directly across the fabric.

Clusters of hand crafted three dimensional flowers in soft blush, lilac, and muted mint are individually arranged along the gown, creating an organic composition that travels from the shoulder through the waist and down the flowing panel. Each flower is shaped with dimensional petals and subtle jeweled centers, adding depth and intricate detail against the vibrant background.

قطعة بالفوشيا الزاهي، مزيّنة بزهور منحوتة يدوياً بألوان الوردي والليلكي والنعناعي، تبدو وكأنها تنمو على الفستان.
