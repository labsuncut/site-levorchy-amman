Aurora Bordeauxأورورا بوردو

Radiant Rebirth

1,400 JOD1,400 د.أ

The gown’s architecture begins with an asymmetric neckline, where one shoulder is delicately draped while the other reveals a crystallized strap, symbolizing balance between fluidity and structure

At the core, a mirrored panel stretches through the torso, hand-embellished with thousands of reflective crystals and metallic fragments. These mirrored details catch every glint of light, creating a living aurora effect that shifts with movement as if the gown itself is awakening

The fitted bodice transitions into flowing silk panels that part gently at the legs, sculpting both motion and grace. The draped sleeve that falls from one arm adds a touch of drama, echoing the elegance of classical couture with a modern Levorchy edge

“Radiant Rebirth” embodies the journey of light rediscovered a vision of renewal, self-realization, and the eternal power of feminine radiance
